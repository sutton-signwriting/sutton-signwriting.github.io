Public guide · Front Office

Research, design, and review before implementation.

The Sutton SignWriting Front Office turns public questions and deliberately supplied material into clear, source-aware proposals for human review.

Visit the public Office See the working method
Core principle Organize first. Decide deliberately. Publish only after approval.

Purpose

A standing organization, not a particular tool.

The Front Office is the durable organizational idea. It coordinates research, planning, product thinking, editorial work, risk review, and decision preparation. Tools and AI products may change without changing that purpose.

Its output is provisional. The Front Office can clarify choices and prepare evidence, but human authority controls decisions, external communication, publication, spending, and implementation.

Working method

Every assignment moves through a visible chain.

01

Request

Name the question, desired outcome, audience, and constraints.

02

Scope

Choose an accountable owner and identify useful collaborators.

03

Evidence

Separate sourced facts, inferences, proposals, and open questions.

04

Draft

Assemble one coherent package with options, risks, and tradeoffs.

05

Review

Challenge accuracy, feasibility, tone, safety, and completeness.

06

Decision

Return the package with the next human decision made explicit.

Public boundary

Useful collaboration depends on explicit limits.

Appropriate inputs

  • Public websites, documentation, code, and issue trackers
  • Material deliberately supplied and cleared for the assignment
  • Questions that benefit from research, comparison, or design

Human-controlled actions

  • Publishing, sending messages, creating accounts, or accepting terms
  • Spending money, merging code, or changing production systems
  • Moving private development information into public work
